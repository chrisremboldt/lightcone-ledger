Certificate number: EXW-0001 (invented), amendment 1 (invented)
Amendment 1: Attachment A now lists the holder's pilots by the identifiers its crew records use. Provision 1 is the only provision this amendment changes.
Holder: Alpha Dispatch, a fictional organization seeded in this repository
Responsible person: Dana Ortiz, a fictional person seeded in this repository
Effective: 2026-09-01 through 2028-08-31 (invented dates)
Operating area: the Canal District delivery area, an invented place
Operations described by the holder: night parcel delivery flights within the operating area
Relief: none. This document grants no relief from any regulation; it exists to exercise Ledger's checks.

SPECIAL PROVISIONS

1. Listed pilots. Only a person listed in Attachment A acts as remote pilot in command. Attachment A lists the holder's source identifiers ALPHA-PILOT-01 and ALPHA-PILOT-03.

2. Listed aircraft. Only an aircraft whose registration number is listed in Attachment B is flown. Attachment B lists FICT-0101 and FICT-0102.

3. Anti-collision lighting. Between sunset and sunrise each aircraft shows anti-collision lighting that the crew confirms at the aircraft, before takeoff, is visible for at least 3 statute miles.

4. Maximum height. No flight is planned higher than 300 feet above ground level.

5. Maximum distance. No flight is planned farther than 1 statute mile from the control station.

6. Crew location. The remote pilot in command stays within the operating area for each flight.

7. Visual observer. A visual observer is assigned to each flight for the whole of its planned window.

8. Notification. The holder notifies the Canal District harbor office at least 24 hours before the first flight of each day.

9. Carriage. No hazardous material is carried.

10. Training. Each remote pilot in command has completed the holder's Night delivery operations course within the previous 12 calendar months.

11. Remote identification. Each aircraft broadcasts remote identification with a built-in or add-on broadcast module.

12. Crew briefing. Before each flight the crew briefing is recorded in the preflight assessment.

13. Certificate at hand. A copy of this certificate is available at the control station during each flight.
