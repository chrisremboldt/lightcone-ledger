EXAMPLE RIDGE SURVEY OPERATIONS MANUAL (INVENTED)

Chapter 7. Flight risk assessment

7.1 Assessment. Before each flight the remote pilot in command answers the five questions of 7.2 and records the answers with the time they were made.

7.2 Points. Each answer scores the points stated here, and the points are added up.
Wind at launch: below 5 m/s, 0 points; 5 m/s to under 8 m/s, 2 points; 8 m/s and above, 4 points.
The site: flown before by this pilot, 0 points; flown before by the operator but not by this pilot, 1 point; new to the operator, 3 points.
The pilot's flights in the last 90 days: fewer than 3, 3 points; 3 to 9, 1 point; 10 or more, 0 points.
People within 30 m of the planned path: no, 0 points; yes, 2 points.
The pilot's hours on duty at takeoff: under 6 h, 0 points; 6 h to under 10 h, 1 point; 10 h and above, 3 points.
When the total is 5 points or more, a second remote pilot reviews the assessment before the flight.
When the total is above the level the chief pilot has set, the chief pilot reviews the assessment before the flight.

7.3 What the total is. The total is a sum of the points above. It is a prompt for a second look, and nothing else.
