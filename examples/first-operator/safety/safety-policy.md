# Safety policy

Example Ridge Survey flies small unmanned aircraft along transmission lines, beyond the pilot's sight. We commit to
the following.

1. Safety comes before the inspection schedule. Any pilot may refuse or stop a flight and will not be questioned for it.
2. We report hazards, mistakes and near misses, our own included. A report made in good faith is never used for
   discipline.
3. We provide the people, training, time and equipment the operation needs, and we do not fly when we cannot.
4. We set safety objectives each year and review them in the management review.
5. Jordan Example is accountable for safety. Rowan Example runs the safety program and may speak to Jordan at any time.

Signed by Jordan Example, accountable executive. Reviewed every year.
