# Code of conduct

Ledger is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This code applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other space for the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Give and take review in good faith: specific, kind and about the change.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, including insults, threats, unwelcome attention, and demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Data in the open

Ledger handles safety records, so this matters here more than in most projects:

- Use only fictional data. The seeded tenants exist for this.
- Never put real operational data, real safety reports, real credentials or personal data about real people in an issue, pull request, comment, screenshot or log.
- Never name a real operator, customer or partner as the subject of an example.

## Reporting a problem

Email team@lightconesystems.com. Tell us what happened, where and when, and who was involved, with links if you have them. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept as confidential as the matter allows. To report a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks it, depending on how serious the matter is and whether it is repeated. They will remove anything that contains real operational or personal data as soon as they see it.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
